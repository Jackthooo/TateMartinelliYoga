My Yoga Styles

I received my YTT 200 Hour Training in Hot Power Yoga, and teach 3 styles:

Power Yoga

A highly physically demanding practice that challenges you to build internal heat in external heat. Expect fast-paced movements, finding your edge, plenty of sweat, and plenty of endorphins released!

Vinyasa

A fluid practice where breath guides movement from one pose to the next. A moving meditation demanding high mental focus and endurance.

Yin

A slower-paced, restorative practice. Yin is mainly practiced with floor poses.

From student to teacher

Hi, I'm Tate! I'm from Northern California, and I've been practicing yoga for six years. When I first started taking yoga classes, I was frustrated trying to master the poses. Once I got stronger at performing poses, I realized the real “work” was needed internally. This is because yoga forces you to become an audience member to your own mind.

As a teacher and forever student, I now practice and preach being gentle and disciplined at the same time. Yoga is about meeting discomfort without immediately trying to escape it. I teach yoga because I get to work alongside others who are discovering their moments of peace, and be the one to deliver that peace through my classes. I work to create a space that honors the fact that our bodies our shifting everyday, a space where we can show up as we are and simply do our best.
