Practice

My Yoga Styles

I received my YTT 200 Hour Training in Hot Power Yoga, but I teach 3 styles:

Power Yoga

A highly physically demanding practice that challenges you to build internal heat in external heat. Expect fast-paced movements, finding your edge, plenty of sweat, and plenty of endorphins released.

Vinyasa

A fluid practice where breath guides movement from one pose to the next. A moving meditation demanding high mental focus, endurance, with continuous shifts in poses.

Yin

A slower-paced, restorative practice submitting to postures for longer periods of time to support muscle recovery and encourage joint mobility. Mainly practiced entirely on the floor, not coming higher than from up on your knees.

My Journey

From student to teacher

Hi, I’m Tate! I’ve been practicing yoga for six years. When I first started my practice, I was solely focused on mastering the poses. I got frustrated, I compared my body and practice to everyone else’s. Out of envy, I kept showing up. The stronger I got at doing the physical poses, the harder going to yoga became. I realized the work was needed in my mind. No one tells you the hardest part of class can be being forced as an audience member of your own mind. I wanted to get my certification so I could teach yoga to others with my own impressions, centered with honesty and patience. I knew I wanted to work in a field where I can be of service to others. I also knew I couldn’t work in healthcare—I’m too queasy—but yoga showed me that I could offer another kind of health and care.

Our bodies are different every day. They change, they have limitations, and they ask different things of us throughout a 24 hour cycle. I work to create a space that honors that: a space where we can show up as we are and simply do our best. To me, yoga is a practice of being gentle with yourself, being disciplined, being present, and meeting discomfort without immediately trying to escape it.
